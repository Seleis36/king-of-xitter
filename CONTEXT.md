# Game Rules Context

This glossary defines the game terms shared by the engine, cards, bots, and UI.

## Language

**Leader**: A playable character represented by a distinct persona, portrait, colour, and voice.

**Player**: A participant in a match who controls one Leader and has Followers, Mandates, and Characters.

**Main Character**: The Player currently occupying Xitter.

**Followers**: A Player's health. A Player with no Followers is suspended and eliminated.

**Mandates**: A Player's victory score. A Player wins by reaching 20 Mandates or by being the last account standing.

**Characters**: A Player's spendable currency, counted in groups of ten.

**Policy**: A persistent power-up kept by a Player.

**Executive Order**: A one-time power-up effect that resolves when the card is bought.

**Suspended**: Eliminated from the match after reaching zero Followers.
